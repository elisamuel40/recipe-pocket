Weeknight Lemon Rice

Servings 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups long-grain rice
  • 2¼ cups water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
  2. Simmer it, covered, for 18 minutes.
  3. Stir in the oil, lemon and parsley.

Install it in about a minute

Recipe Pocket isn’t on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it through Chrome’s developer mode.

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it into a folder you’ll keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the folder.
