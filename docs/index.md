<ul class="promises">
<li><strong>No account.</strong> Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.</li>
<li><strong>No analytics.</strong> The extension makes no network requests of its own: no telemetry, no ads, no tracking.</li>
<li><strong>Saved on your device.</strong> Recipes live in this browser profile and go nowhere else. Export a backup file whenever you like.</li>
</ul>

## What it does
- Reads the recipe on the page you're on, only when you click its icon.
- Scales servings with exact fractions and converts between US and metric units.
- Prints clean pages or saves them as PDF, with an easy-to-follow cook mode.
- Keeps a library with search, collections, tags and notes, plus shopping lists.
